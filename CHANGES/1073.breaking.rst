Percent-encoded reserved characters (``$``, ``'``, ``(``, ``)``, ``*``,
``,``, ``/``, ``:``, ``@``) in query keys and values passed as a mapping,
sequence of pairs, or keyword arguments to :meth:`URL.build() <yarl.URL.build>`,
:meth:`~yarl.URL.with_query`, and :meth:`~yarl.URL.extend_query`, and in
queries rebuilt by :meth:`~yarl.URL.update_query` and
:meth:`~yarl.URL.without_query_params` (which re-encode retained parameters,
and :class:`str` arguments for ``update_query``); ``!`` and ``?`` are still
left unencoded, as are literal query strings passed to
``URL.build(query_string=...)``, ``URL.with_query(str)``, and
``URL.extend_query(str)`` -- by :user:`pctablet505`.
