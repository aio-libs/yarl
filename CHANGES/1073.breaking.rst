Percent-encoded reserved characters (``$``, ``'``, ``(``, ``)``, ``*``,
``,``, ``/``, ``:``, ``@``) in query keys and values passed as a mapping,
sequence of pairs, or keyword arguments to :meth:`URL.build() <yarl.URL.build>`,
:meth:`~yarl.URL.with_query`, and :meth:`~yarl.URL.extend_query`, and in
queries produced by :meth:`~yarl.URL.update_query` (which re-encodes the
entire query string, including untouched parameters and :class:`str`
arguments); ``!`` and ``?`` are still left unencoded, as are literal query
strings passed to ``URL.build(query_string=...)``, ``URL.with_query(str)``,
and ``URL.extend_query(str)`` -- by :user:`pctablet505`.
