Adopted :pep:`639` license metadata: the license is now declared as the
SPDX expression ``Apache-2.0`` and ``license-files`` moved to the
``[project]`` table, which bumps the build-time requirement to
``setuptools >= 77.0``. This supersedes the ``>= 47`` lower bound
restored in :issue:`1764`, since the SPDX form is not understood by
older ``setuptools``. Built distributions now carry
``License-Expression`` and ship :file:`LICENSE` and :file:`NOTICE` under
:file:`*.dist-info/licenses/` -- by :user:`aiolibsbot`.
