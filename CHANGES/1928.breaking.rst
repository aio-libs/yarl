Stopped decoding percent-encoded reserved characters, such as ``%3A``,
``%40``, ``%3D`` or ``%28``, when parsing the path of a URL, and stopped
decoding them when :meth:`URL.join() <yarl.URL.join>` merges the base
path; only unreserved characters are decoded now, as
:rfc:`3986#section-6.2.2.2` requires. ``URL("http://h/a%3Ab")`` is no
longer equal to ``URL("http://h/a:b")``, and its ``str()`` and
:attr:`~yarl.URL.raw_path` keep the escape, while :attr:`~yarl.URL.path`
is unchanged
-- by :user:`asvetlov`.
