Kept percent-encoded reserved characters, such as ``%2F``, ``%3F`` or
``%27``, encoded when parsing and requoting the query and fragment of a
URL, instead of decoding them to the literal character, as
:rfc:`3986#section-6.2.2.2` requires. ``URL("http://h/?a%2Fb")`` is no
longer equal to ``URL("http://h/?a/b")``, and its ``str()``,
:attr:`~yarl.URL.raw_query_string` and :attr:`~yarl.URL.raw_fragment` keep
the escape, while :attr:`~yarl.URL.query` and :attr:`~yarl.URL.fragment`
are unchanged
-- by :user:`BlueX888`.
