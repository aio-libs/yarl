Kept the brackets of an IPvFuture address (:rfc:`3986#section-3.2.2`),
such as ``http://[v1.x]/``, in RFC 3986 mode: they were dropped, which
turned the host into a *reg-name* and changed the meaning of the URL.
The ``host`` and ``raw_host`` of such a URL keep the brackets too.
WHATWG mode, whose standard has no IPvFuture, now rejects one
-- by :user:`asvetlov`.
