Added :func:`~yarl.query_to_pairs`, a faster replacement for
:func:`urllib.parse.parse_qsl` with ``keep_blank_values=True`` that also
takes ``max_fields`` and ``encoding`` arguments. :attr:`URL.query
<yarl.URL.query>` and :meth:`URL.update_query() <yarl.URL.update_query>`
now match :func:`urllib.parse.parse_qsl` again by skipping empty fields and
replacing invalid percent-encoded UTF-8 with ``U+FFFD``
-- by :user:`bdraco`.
